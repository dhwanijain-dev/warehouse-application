# User Guide - Warehouse Scan

## 1. Login
- Enter your Username and Password.
- Tap **Login**.
- *(Optional)* Long-press the app logo on the login screen to access Admin Settings and update server endpoints.

## 2. Home Screen
Choose between two main workflows:
- **Location Setting**: Capture batches for a specific Rack, Zone, and Setting and email them.
- **Dispatch Verification**: Upload a dispatch TXT file and verify floor scans against it.

## 3. Workflow 1: Location Setting
1. Enter **Rack**, **Zone**, and **Setting**.
2. Scan fabric roll barcodes with the hardware scanner or enter them manually.
3. Review the live list of scanned batches.
4. Tap **Submit & Email Session** to generate the TXT file and email it to your profile email.

## 4. Workflow 2: Dispatch Verification
1. Tap **Upload TXT** to select your dispatch file.
2. View live metrics: **Uploaded**, **Matched**, **Unmatched**, and **Pending**.
3. Scan floor material; the app instantly validates each scan against the uploaded list.
4. Export pending or unmatched reports via the share sheet.
